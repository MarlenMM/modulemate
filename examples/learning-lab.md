# Learning Lab — original fictional course

This is an original demonstration document written for ModuleMate. It is not
university course material. All dates and rules below are fictional examples.

## Retrieval practice
Retrieval practice means trying to recall an idea before looking at the answer.
After an attempt, compare your answer with the source and correct any gaps.
Rereading can feel familiar without showing whether you can recall an idea.

## Spacing
Spacing means revisiting material across separate study sessions. In this demo,
study sessions are 25 minutes long with a 5-minute break. A useful review card
asks one focused question and keeps the answer short enough to check quickly.

## Interleaving
Interleaving alternates different kinds of problems in a practice session.
Blocked practice repeats one problem type before moving to the next. Compare
which strategy you need to choose, rather than memorizing only the procedure.

## Example assessment
The fictional Learning Lab portfolio contains three reflection notes and a
five-minute presentation. The portfolio is due on 20 November 2027. The
presentation is worth 40 percent and the reflection notes are worth 60 percent.
Students may use this study assistant for practice; this fictional policy says
nothing about the rules of a real institution or course.
